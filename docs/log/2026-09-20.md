## DEM resampling bug — fake mountain ridge (ADR-0021)
- Symptom: the compact district rendered a wall of sharp "shark-fin" mountains
  and steep hills; buildings looked stretched/squashed and road ribbons draped
  over the fake slopes read as big black patches.
- Cause: `scripts/fetch-dem.ts` used `readRasters({ bbox, width, height })`, which
  mis-samples on this COG — it invented a ~455 m ridge along the NE edge where
  the real elevation is ~5 m.
- Fix: read a native-resolution pixel `{ window }` and box-filter down to the
  target grid ourselves. District range is now **1.07 … 58.27 m** (was
  −0.8 … 455.7 m). Cross-checked with Open-Meteo (Copernicus GLO-90) point
  queries (~3–36 m across the box).
- Follow-up: with the fake slopes gone, the leftover "black hole" at Cheragi
  Pahar was just near-black asphalt at a grazing angle; asphalt is now mid
  slate-grey (`0x51515a`).

## Road normals pointed down (GTAO made roads black)
- `Roads.addRibbon` emitted a winding whose computed normals all faced **down**
  (verified: 3082/3082 normals with `y ≈ -1`). GTAO consumes the normal buffer, so
  every road was treated as fully occluded and shaded to near-black — boosting the
  hemisphere light had no effect.
- Reversed the index order to `a, c, b, b, c, d`; all 3082 normals now face up and
  roads render as readable pavement (compare `docs/images/spawn-junction-fixed.png`).
